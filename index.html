How to play
  1. Roll the dice.
  2. Shut any open tiles that add up to the roll. Roll 8? Shut the 8, or 7 + 1, or 5 + 2 + 1, and so on.
  3. Keep rolling and shutting. When no open tiles can make the roll, your turn ends.
  4. Your score is the sum of the tiles still open. Lower is better. Shut every tile and you've shut the box (score 0).
  5. With friends, everyone plays a full box in turn. Lowest score wins the round.

Keyboard: number keys pick tiles (0 = 10, - = 11, = = 12), Space or Enter rolls or shuts, N starts a new solo game.
